Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30 · This page is an editable draft. Replace the [bracketed] placeholders with your own information, and have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before publishing.

1. How payments work (important)

Your payment is collected and processed by Creem / Paddle acting as the Merchant of Record (MoR) on our behalf. You do not pay us directly, and the invoice is issued by the MoR. Refunds are therefore returned through the MoR's payment system to the original method, and we cooperate in processing them.

2. Refund exceptions for digital services

Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive, digital content (such as an activated scan / monitoring service) is generally considered performed upon delivery and is not subject to a 14-day no-questions-asked refund. We do support refunds in the following cases:

  • Within [14] days of payment, you have never actually used any paid feature;
  • A duplicate charge or technical failure meant the service was not actually delivered;
  • We unilaterally cease providing the Service in your region.

3. How to request a refund

  • Email [billing@yourdomain.com] with subject "Refund Request" and your order number (found in the receipt email sent by the MoR);
  • Or initiate a refund request directly from your Creem / Paddle customer portal.

4. Processing time

Once approved by us and the MoR, funds are returned to your original payment method, usually within [5–10] business days (depending on the MoR and your card / payment institution).

5. Subscription cancellation (not a refund)

Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals, but the current paid period remains usable until its expiry, after which it ends automatically without an automatic refund.

6. Contact

For refund questions, contact: [billing@yourdomain.com].
